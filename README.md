# Interpretable Colon Cancer Detection Using Advanced Deep Learning and XAI Techniques
Colon Cancer detection using Advanced Deep Learning models. Models used are ResNet50 and DenseNet with Attention layer. Explainable AI (XAI) techniques used for interpretations.

## Overview
 Colon cancer is the third most common cancer worldwide, accounting for approxi
mately 10% of all cancer cases. With nearly 935,000 deaths reported worldwide, it has
 become the second most fatal cancer. Reliable diagnostic tools are crucial for improving
 results. The proposed hybrid framework of attention based transfer learning with XAI for
 detecting colon cancer in histopathology images, with an emphasis on the use of explain
able artificial intelligence (XAI) techniques to interpret the results. This study compares 6
 advanced models: DenseNet121 with Spatial Attention (SA), DenseNet121 with Squeeze
Excitation layer (SE), ResNet50 with Spatial Attention, ResNet50 with Squeeze-Excitation
 layer, MobileNetV3 and DualAttention ResNet. Four different XAI techniques, Local Inter
pretable Model-Agnostic Explanations (LIME), Gradient-weighted Class Activation Map
ping (Grad-CAM), Grad-CAM++, andOcclusionSensitivity are used to interpret the model.
 Among these, Dual Attention ResNet has shown the best performance, achieving an ac
curacy of 91%, precision of 91%, ROCof97%, f1-score of 91%, specificity of 95% andsen
sitivity (recall) of 91%. In XAI, Grad-CAM++ emerged as the most effective technique for
 clear and interpretable visualizations. These results highlight the potential of combining ad
vanced architectures with XAI techniques to enhance trust and transparency in AI-powered
 diagnostic systems, ultimately fostering confidence among radiologists and physicians.
